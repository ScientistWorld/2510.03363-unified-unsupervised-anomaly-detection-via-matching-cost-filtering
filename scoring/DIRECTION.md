# Research Direction

Work within unsupervised visual anomaly detection based on matching a test image against normal references. Valid improvements should refine how anomaly evidence is represented, filtered, calibrated, or trained under normal-only supervision.

Do not switch to supervised defect segmentation, train on real anomaly masks, replace the task with a generic classifier, or use external anomaly datasets to tune the benchmark. The purpose of this gym is to study whether matching-cost representations can be made cleaner and more discriminative under the paper's problem setting.
