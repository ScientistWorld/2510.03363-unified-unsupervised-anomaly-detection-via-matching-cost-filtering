# Research Direction

Work within unsupervised visual anomaly detection for image-level detection and pixel-level localization under normal-only training. Valid improvements should refine how anomaly evidence is represented, calibrated, or trained while preserving the benchmark's data and output contract.

Do not switch to supervised defect segmentation, train on real anomaly masks, replace the task with a generic classifier, or use external anomaly datasets to tune the benchmark. The purpose of this gym is to study compact normal-only visual anomaly detection under the paper's problem setting.
